Cancellation & Refund Policy

Last updated: [add date when published]

Note to the site owner: this is a starting draft, not a finished legal document. Have it reviewed by a qualified lawyer or CA before relying on it — refund policies affect your Razorpay approval too.

1. Registration fees

The one-time ₹100 worker registration fee and ₹50 customer registration fee are non-refundable once your account or application is successfully created, because this fee covers our verification and platform costs, not a specific service.

2. When a refund does apply

If a payment was deducted from your account but your worker application or customer account was not successfully created due to a technical error, you're entitled to a full refund. Contact us with your payment ID (shown on screen if this happens) and we'll process it within 5–7 business days.

If you were charged twice for the same registration by mistake, the duplicate charge will be refunded in full.

3. Service requests

Submitting a request to a worker through the app is free — there is no charge for requesting or messaging a worker. Any charge for the actual work performed (labour, materials) is agreed directly between you and the worker and is not currently processed through Sahi Kaamwala.

5. Processing time

Approved refunds are processed back to your original payment method within 5–7 business days, depending on your bank or UPI provider.

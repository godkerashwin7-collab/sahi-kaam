Cancellation & Refund Policy

1. Worker onboarding fee

The one-time ₹100 worker onboarding fee is non-refundable once a worker's account or application is successfully created, because it covers ID collection and platform listing costs, not a specific service. Creating a customer account is free — there is no customer registration fee.

2. When an onboarding-fee refund does apply

If a payment was deducted but a worker's application was not successfully created due to a technical error, or you were charged twice by mistake, you're entitled to a full refund of the duplicate/failed charge. Contact us with your payment ID (shown on screen if this happens) and we'll process it within 5–7 business days.

3. Booking payments (paying online for a job)

When you pay online at the time of booking, the amount shown is an estimate — the professional may revise it based on the actual job once they inspect the work.

  • Cancel before a professional is assigned: full refund.
  • Cancel after a professional is assigned but before work starts: full refund, minus any visit charge the professional has already incurred travelling to your location, if applicable.
  • Cancel after work has started: refund only for the unstarted portion of the job; you pay for work already completed and materials already used.
  • Pay-cash-later bookings: since no online payment was taken, there is nothing to refund — simply cancel the booking before the professional arrives.

To cancel a booking, contact us with your booking details as early as possible.

4. Service quality issues

If the work performed does not match what was agreed, contact us within 48 hours with details and photos where possible. We'll review the case with the professional and may arrange a partial refund, a re-visit, or another resolution depending on what happened.

6. Processing time

Approved refunds are processed back to your original payment method within 5–7 business days, depending on your bank or UPI provider.

1. What Sahi Kaamwala is

Sahi Kaamwala is a platform that connects customers in Mumbai with independent home-service professionals — currently carpenters, electricians, painters, plumbers, and cleaners. Workers listed on the platform are independent professionals, not employees of Sahi Kaamwala. Community support categories (women aid, medical aid, old age assistance) are shown for information and genuine public emergency helplines only, and are not yet live booking services — we will update these terms before turning on booking for those categories.

2. Accounts and eligibility

You must be at least 18 years old to create a customer account or register as a worker. You're responsible for keeping your login details secure and for all activity under your account.

3. Registration fees

Creating a customer account is free. Workers pay a one-time ₹100 onboarding fee to be listed on the platform — this is a one-time charge, not a recurring subscription. See our Cancellation & Refund Policy for when a refund applies.

4. Verification — what we actually do

We verify a phone number by one-time code, and we collect (but do not independently verify against government databases) an ID number from workers at sign-up. We do not currently perform criminal background checks, formal skills training, or insurance coverage. We'll update this page the moment any of that changes.

5. Staying on the platform

To keep everyone safe, contact between customers and workers happens through Sahi Kaamwala's chat and request system, not by sharing personal phone numbers directly. Please don't ask other users to move the conversation off-platform before a booking is confirmed.

6. Service quality and disputes

Sahi Kaamwala facilitates the connection between customers and workers but is not itself performing the household or care service. If you have an issue with a job, please contact us so we can help resolve it — we take complaints seriously and may remove a worker from the platform for repeated or serious issues.

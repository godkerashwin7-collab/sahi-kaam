Who we are

Building Mumbai's Trust for Over 25 Years

We are Naresh Construction — proudly Mumbaikars, proudly local, and proudly serving our community for over 25 years. What began as a journey in the civil and construction industry has grown into something much bigger: a trusted name built through hard work, quality, commitment, and the relationships we've created with our customers across Mumbai.

For more than two decades, we've helped build, repair, improve, and transform homes and spaces. Every project, every customer, and every challenge has taught us one important thing — a home is more than just walls and a roof; it is where life happens. We've also proudly served the PWD (Public Works Department) with full-fledged support over the years.

Today, we're bringing our 25 years of experience and trusted reputation into the digital world with one simple mission: to make reliable home and civil services easily accessible to every Mumbaikar — quickly, professionally, and right at their doorstep. Whether it's a small repair, renovation, construction work, plumbing, electrical services, painting, or any other home requirement, we want every customer to have one trusted place they can turn to.

Because we understand Mumbai — its homes, its buildings, its people, and its fast-paced life. And most importantly, we understand that when something goes wrong at home, you don't want to spend hours searching for someone reliable. You need trusted help, and you need it quickly. That's why we're here.

Common questions

Frequently asked questions

How are professionals verified?

Every professional on Sahi Kaamwala goes through ID verification before their first job. We're working towards uniforms and formal licensing so you always know who's at your door.

What happens if I don't like the service?

Message us on WhatsApp or call us right after the job and we'll work with you to make it right. See our refund policy for details.

Can I cancel my booking?

Yes — contact us via call or WhatsApp as early as possible before the scheduled time. See our terms for the full cancellation policy.

When do I pay?

You can pay online at booking or choose "Pay Cash After Service" — you only pay once the work is confirmed done.

Is there a visit or inspection charge?

The prices shown are starting estimates for the category. The professional confirms the exact price with you before starting any work — no surprise bills.

Can I book multiple services together?

Yes — select as many services as you need across categories and submit them as one request. One visit can often cover more than one job.

What if something gets damaged during the job?

Let us know immediately via call or WhatsApp with photos. We'll look into it with the professional involved and work towards a fair resolution.

Do you offer emergency bookings?

Right now, submit a request any time via "Submit a Request — Anytime" and we'll do our best to match you quickly based on professional availability in your area.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: [add date when published]

Note to the site owner: this is a starting draft, not a finished legal document. Have it reviewed by a qualified lawyer before relying on it — especially since this platform collects government ID numbers (Aadhaar/PAN) and processes payments.

1. What we collect

From customers: name, email, phone number (verified by one-time code), address entered when requesting a service, and date/time preferences for a booking.

From workers applying to join: name, email, phone number, address, area of work, and a government ID (Aadhaar, PAN, Driving License, or Passport).

We do not collect or store your card, UPI, or bank details ourselves — payments are processed directly by our payment partner, Razorpay.

2. How we use it

  • To verify you are a real, reachable person before allowing contact between customers and workers
  • To review worker applications before listing someone on the platform
  • To connect a customer's service request with the right worker
  • To respond to support requests via chat, call, or WhatsApp

3. Who we share it with

Payment details are shared with Razorpay solely to process your registration fee. Worker applications, including ID numbers, are reviewed only by our internal team — never displayed publicly or sent to other users. Chat messages within the app are visible to the customer and worker in that conversation.

4. Data retention

We keep account and application data for as long as your account is active, or as long as needed to resolve a dispute or meet a legal requirement. You can request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us (see Section 7).

5. Your rights

You can ask us to show you what data we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, you're entitled to these requests being handled within a reasonable time.

6. Cookies and local storage

We use your browser's local storage to remember things like your selected services and login session — this stays on your device and isn't sold or shared with advertisers. We don't run third-party ad tracking on this site.

7. Contact us

For any privacy question or data request: call us or message us on WhatsApp.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
